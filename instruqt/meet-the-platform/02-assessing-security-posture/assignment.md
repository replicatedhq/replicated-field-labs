One of the most important aspects of the Replicated Platform is that is
provides both you and your customer with a shared view of your application's
security posture. This view is unique in that shows each of you the scan
results, SBOMs, etc. in context of your customer's running instances and each
of your released versions.

> Note: During this step they'll review the Security Center as a customer and
> we'll also have them discover that the same information is avialable about
