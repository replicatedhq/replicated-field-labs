After your customer has reviewed your security reports, it's time for them to
install your software. They being this process in the Enterprise Portal, where
they access your installation instructions. As they advance through the
process, the Vendor Portal will reflect their progress back to you so you can
see how they're doing.

> Note: This step will take them from accessing the install instructions to
